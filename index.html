Privacy Policy for Catzip

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Catzip is a mobile game developed by Dev Hansen. Your privacy is important, and Catzip is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Information Collection

Catzip does not collect, transmit, or store personal information.

The game does not currently use:

  • User accounts or login systems
  • Advertising
  • Analytics or tracking services
  • Location data
  • Personal identifiers

Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Third-Party Services

Catzip does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Catzip does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Catzip's features or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Catzip Google Play listing.
